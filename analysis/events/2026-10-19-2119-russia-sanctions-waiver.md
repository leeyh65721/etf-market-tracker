# US temporary waiver of Russia sanctions: oil-supply counterweight — 2026-10-19

## Public event and timing
- On **2026-10-16 (US time)**, President Trump signed an executive order waiving key sanctions/tariffs under the Graham Act, according to Reuters. Secretary of State Rubio said the administration would take up to **60 days** to assess the economic impact. This is a **temporary waiver/review**, not verified permanent sanctions removal.
- Reuters reported a separate proposed diesel-export arrangement, initially approximately **2.3 million barrels this month**, potentially rising to approximately **7.6 million barrels monthly**; projected flows are **not** verified deliveries.
- Event was publicly reported over Oct 16-19. This is **not** a newly occurring Oct 19 evening event.

- These observations **do not prove** that waiver alone caused all market moves.

## Why this is distinct from the existing event
- The Oct 19 05:35 KST alert already recorded an Oct 18 Ukrainian drone strike/fire at Russian St Petersburg oil facilities; export losses from that strike remained **unverified**.

- Counterforces: Hormuz shipping disruption, high insurance/freight, attacks on Russian energy assets, possible US reversal after 60-day review, and incomplete pass-through to consumer prices.
