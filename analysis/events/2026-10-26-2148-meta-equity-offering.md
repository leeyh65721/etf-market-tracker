# Meta formally launches $35bn equity offerings; outlines potential $60bn ATM — 2026-10-26

**Checked:** 2026-10-26 21:48 KST (12:48 UTC). **Event:** Meta announcement dated 2026-10-26 U.S. local time; Reuters report 11:07 UTC. **Scope:** Public-market research only; no personal account information.

## Verified new fact (distinct from earlier AI-funding concerns)
- Meta **launched** concurrent underwritten public offerings totaling **approximately $35bn**, consisting of **$22.5bn Class A common shares** offered under forward sale agreements and **approximately $12.5bn mandatory-convertible preferred depositary shares**. The company states it does not initially receive proceeds from the common-stock forward purchasers; launch is not equivalent to proceeds already raised.
- Meta **plans** a separate **up to $60bn at-the-market (ATM)** offering linked to its new Class C stock. It has not entered an ATM agreement and explicitly says establishment, timing, terms and actual sales are uncertain. **Up to $95bn** is a potential financing plan, not $95bn completed fundraising.
- Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom expressed **non-binding aggregate interest of up to $5bn** in the Class A offering, per Reuters; not completed purchases or orders for chips.
- Meta reiterates **2026 capex $135bn–$145bn**, $97bn operating cash flow for first three quarters, ~$82bn cash/securities at end of Q3 and investment-grade Aa3/AA- ratings in its announcement. Reuters reports Q3 free cash flow **negative $2.12bn**, while revenue **$64.60bn** and Q4 revenue guidance **$73bn–$76bn**.
- **Difference vs Oct 22/23/24 alerts:** Prior records covered Meta capex worries, negative FCF, AI-sector selloff and widening global credit spreads. **Formal launch and disclosed financing structure/size on Oct 26** is a new issuer action; do not re-label old SOXX drop, CPI or previously known guidance as new.
