# Samsung Electronics 2026 Q3 final segment results and 2026 capex plan — 2026-10-29 KST

**Checked:** 2026-10-29 10:39 KST. **Official release:** 2026-10-29 KST, final Q3 results (quarter ended 2026-09-30). Public-market research only; no personal account data.

## New verified evidence vs prior research
- The **KRW 107.4 trillion group operating profit** and approximately **KRW 195 trillion revenue** were **already announced as preliminary guidance on 2026-10-08**. These headline numbers are NOT a fresh upside earnings surprise. Final reported group revenue is KRW **195.3 trillion**, operating profit **107.4 trillion**, +14% and +20% quarter-on-quarter respectively.
- **New final segment disclosure:** Device Solutions (semiconductors) revenue **KRW 147.1 trillion**, operating profit **KRW 106.5 trillion**; Device eXperience revenue **KRW 52.1 trillion**, operating loss **KRW 0.8 trillion**. Segment figures are not necessarily additive to consolidated group results due to eliminations and other divisions.
- Samsung reports materially expanded **HBM4** shipments and server SSD demand; foundry improvement on HBM base dies and advanced nodes, and continued orders involving 2nm HPC clients. The release does not provide verified unit shipments, companywide chip inventory turns, or binding multi-year wafer volumes; do not infer these.
- **2026 full-year capex plan KRW 84.4 trillion**, including **KRW 76.8 trillion in Device Solutions** and KRW 3.9 trillion in display. This is a spending **plan**, not completed cash capex or guaranteed orders for US semiconductor ETF constituents.
- **Forward-looking, not realized:** Samsung expects Q4 semiconductor growth; 2027 memory demand above supply, HBM4E mass production and expanded HBM4, DDR5 and SSD sales, and advanced foundry ramp including Taylor, Texas 2nm AI/HPC. The direction broadly agrees with SK Hynix's previously recorded Oct 28 strong memory-demand outlook; **not an independent new sector-wide demand shock**.
- FX translation/transaction effects reportedly reduced Q3 group operating profit by roughly KRW 10 trillion quarter-on-quarter; the mobile/device division reported a loss despite chip strength. Countercase to interpreting the headline profit as universal strength.

## Transmission and urgent threshold
- Positive semiconductor chain: high actual memory earnings and planned foundry/memory capex -> potential sustained demand for equipment, test and selected suppliers -> supportive context for SOXX, subject to constituent exposure and purchase timing.
- Negative/countercase: capex and new capacity may eventually ease scarcity, compress memory pricing/margins, and raise depreciation; customer hyperscaler financing pressure, elevated US nominal/real yields, energy costs, currency and foundry execution remain risks. The official press release does **not** prove US SOXX member order books or cash flows improved today.
- Prior event research already documented 2026-10-28 SK Hynix results, FOMC hold, Microsoft/Alphabet cash-flow divergence, KLA guidance. This filing is **new final segment/capex evidence**, not a new urgent ETF allocation trigger. **No immediate ETF buy/sell, target-weight change or trade.**
- Escalate only if subsequent independently verified hyperscaler order cuts, simultaneous material semiconductor selling and widening HY/IG credit spreads, financing stress, or unexpected Samsung capex cuts show the demand/capital cycle changing. Reassess on official shipment, order and utilization numbers, not just qualitative 'demand strong' claims.
