## Mechanism, countercase, classification and next check
- **Risk transmission:** expanding hyperscaler capex above internal quarterly cash generation -> more debt/equity/lease financing -> dilution and cost-of-capital sensitivity in a high-yield/high-oil environment -> valuation risk for AI infrastructure buyers and semiconductor suppliers (SOXX) even if orders remain strong. Alphabet after-hours ~8% drop is a single issuer reaction, not proof of sector-wide capitulation.
- **Counterevidence:** Alphabet Cloud sales +103%, operating income +30%, TTM FCF positive and substantial reported financial assets; Microsoft Azure +48% and positive cash FCF; KLA actual revenue/FCF and next-quarter guidance increased. Strong demand could sustain chip orders despite financing pressure. Microsoft and Alphabet marketable securities, accounting, lease and financing definitions are not directly comparable.
- **Classification:** important new **financing/FCF risk evidence with strong operating-demand countercase**; urgent near-term watch for spillover, **not confirmed structural collapse or forced immediate portfolio reallocation**. No new ETF buy recommendation or target weights; defer routine weights to weekly review unless additional verified systemic/issuer deterioration.
- **Escalate if** 29 Oct regular market shows persistent broad semiconductors/megacap selling plus deteriorating HY/IG OAS or financing access; multiple hyperscalers cut capex/orders or report sustained cash deficits and punitive funding; KLA guidance weakens; real yields spike.
- **De-escalate if** Alphabet explains sustainable cash conversion and orderly financing, next-quarter FCF improves, Microsoft guidance/other hyperscalers sustain cash generation, KLA orders and guidance hold, credit remains orderly and sector price breadth stabilizes.
