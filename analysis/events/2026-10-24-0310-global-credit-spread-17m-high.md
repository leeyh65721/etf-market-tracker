## Mechanisms and conditional risk
1. Widening cross-market corporate spreads => costlier AI infrastructure debt refinancing/issuance and more stringent valuation of cash-intensive growth firms => potential semiconductor/technology ETF pressure over coming months. This is **credit repricing**, not proof of actual defaults or a funding-market freeze.
2. Renewed oil escalation => inflation and monetary-policy uncertainty, potentially offsetting falling safe-haven government yields. Higher Brent is not proof of a prolonged physical shortage.
3. French sovereign yields and credit risk can amplify cross-border financial risk if bank/funding markets show contagion; latest evidence shows French yields **easing on the day**, not accelerating.

## Assessment, countercase and escalation/retraction conditions
**Assessment:** NEW meaningful cross-market risk evidence; **elevated caution, not yet confirmed systemic liquidity crisis**. Reassess in hours/days rather than waiting solely for routine weekly review, but **no immediate target ETF weight change or trade instruction** without confirmation of sustained U.S. IG/HY OAS widening, failed issuance, bank funding impairment, durable export loss, or new adverse issuer orders/guidance.

**Escalate** if U.S. IG/HY OAS and funding markets independently confirm broadening stress, if new issuer guidance shows AI capex/orders cut, or if measurable Gulf export losses persist. **Downgrade/retract** if Bloomberg global spreads tighten, U.S. credit remains orderly, Brent reverses on restored shipping, and semiconductor earnings/cash-flow expectations stabilize.
