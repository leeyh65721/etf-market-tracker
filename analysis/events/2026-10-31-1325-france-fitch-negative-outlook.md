# France: Fitch sovereign outlook revised to negative, A+ affirmed — 2026-10-31 KST

## New verified change vs pre-existing risks
- **New action:** Fitch Ratings **changed France's long-term sovereign rating outlook from stable to negative while AFFIRMING the A+ issuer rating**. This is **not a rating downgrade, default, or confirmed failed sovereign bond auction**. Reuters directly reports and quotes Fitch; WSJ independently describes the action. The primary Fitch rating-action page was not directly retrieved in this check; the French Treasury ratings table still displays the older Aug 28 stable outlook and is not used to overrule the newer published action.
- **Reasons:** Weaker economic growth, higher borrowing costs, widening fiscal deficit, political fragmentation, and difficulty delivering durable consolidation. Fitch projects France's public debt around **124% of GDP in 2028**, up from **115.7% in 2025**; this is a forecast, not an observed 2028 outcome.
- **Separately released Oct 30 macro evidence:** France Q3 GDP **-0.1% quarter on quarter** versus +0.1% consensus; October harmonized inflation **3.8%** (Reuters). These data predate the Fitch decision and must not be relabeled as consequences of the rating action.
- **Prior risks, not new:** French sovereign yields above 5% earlier in October, Moody's Oct 23 Aa3/negative outlook affirmation, European political/fiscal tensions, Oct 23 global corporate spread widening and previously reported U.S. tech/AI selloff. This archive records **only Fitch's distinct outlook change**.

- **Classification:** A meaningful **new sovereign credit-outlook event** in the euro area's second-largest economy; worthy of archival tracking, **not** evidence of an immediate eurozone funding crisis. **Urgency:** watch closely at the next European market open; **no emergency U.S. ETF target-weight change** before corroborating transmission. Existing Oct 31 weekly risk review remains the baseline.
