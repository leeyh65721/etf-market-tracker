- Systems revenue **$5.733B**, versus **$4.250B QoQ** and **$3.548B YoY**; customer support/other $2.487B versus $2.472B QoQ.

- Quarter ending Dec 27 guidance: revenue **$8.60B ± $0.40B**, non-GAAP EPS **$2.33 ± $0.15**; guidance midpoint only slightly ahead of cited street estimates (~$8.54–8.57B). China **35%** of quarterly sales, Korea 20%, Taiwan 18%.
- A contemporaneous market report described LRCX down around **5–6% after hours** despite record earnings, consistent with high expectations; this is a delayed indicative snapshot, not a final closing price or proof of a broad sector selloff.

### Meta (META): hyperscaler spending and cash-flow tradeoff
- Q3 revenue **$64.601B (+26% YoY)**; quarterly capex **$34.91B**.
- 2026 capex guidance **$135–145B**, revised from **$130–145B**: the **floor rises $5B**, but the upper bound is unchanged; not a $5B increase to the whole range.
- Q3 free cash flow approximately **-$2.12B**; the quarterly GAAP earnings figure is distorted by a large legal settlement charge. A single-quarter negative FCF does not establish inability to finance the buildout.

## Interpretation, countercase and decision
- **Incremental evidence**: Lam's recognized equipment shipments and TXN's realized analog sales add two different types of actual revenue evidence to prior SMCI preliminary server numbers. Meta's spending floor adds a buyer-side investment signal.
- **Countercase**: Lam's guide barely beat expectations and after-hours share weakness suggests much growth priced in; China concentration and export controls remain material. Meta's negative FCF and elevated financing rates keep capex sustainability in question. Results from these firms do not prove SOXX constituent-wide bookings or guarantee price recovery.
- **ETF channel**: equipment (LRCX), analog (TXN), and cloud buyer capex (META) can support the chip supply-chain revenue thesis, while ~5% nominal 10Y rates, high crude, financing stress and elevated valuation can offset fundamental improvements.
- **Hourly emergency decision: no notification and no urgent allocation change**. Positive but mixed earnings are appropriate for the daily/weekly review, not a mandate to chase a rebound. No new ETF recommendation or trades.
- **Escalation/withdrawal conditions**: significant verified supplier order cancellations, materially reduced hyperscaler capex, fresh systemic credit/liquidity shock, unexpected export controls, or synchronized material cross-asset repricing. Conversely, confirmation from multiple customers, cash conversion and broader spreads stabilizing strengthens the benign interpretation.
