## Interpretation, counterevidence, pathways and limits
- **New signal:** US high-yield and investment-grade bond risk premia both **widened on Oct 30**, contemporaneous with the already documented SOXX **-8.24%** day and AI infrastructure free-cash-flow concerns. Cross-market risk repricing is independently confirmed. **Temporal coincidence does not establish one-way causality.** Do not re-alert the old CPI or SOXX decline as new events.
- **Potential pathway:** wider HY/IG spreads -> increased marginal financing/refinancing costs and tighter AI infrastructure project economics -> possible pressure on capex-funded hardware demand, valuation multiples and semiconductor ETFs. This is a **risk pathway**, not a verified cut in orders, default wave, or liquidity failure.
- **Countercase:** HY **3.44%** and IG **0.94%** are higher than the prior day but do not alone show an extraordinary systemic credit dislocation; SOFR changed only +1 bp. Nov 2 Reuters at **10:02 EST** reported S&P500 **+0.58%**, Nasdaq **+0.72%**, NYSE advance/decline **3.13:1**, Nasdaq **2.08:1**, and **seven of 11** S&P sectors positive. These are **Nov 2 intraday** figures and not synchronous with Oct 30 credit closes. Alphabet's Nov 2 €6bn bond offering received **>€17bn indicated orders**, but final terms/settlement were unverified. Oil's >6% decline reflected *existing* Iran diplomacy hopes, not a confirmed peace deal.
- **Market risk assessment:** meaningful **credit repricing / early contagion evidence**, **not yet a confirmed systemic credit or funding shock** and **not a new emergency ETF target-weight change trigger**. Previous Oct 31 weekly semiconductor concentration-risk reduction review stays active; routine allocation work belongs to the weekly review unless credit stress escalates. No new ETF recommendation and no trades.
- **Escalate promptly if:** further HY/IG OAS jumps across subsequent closes, multiple corporate issuance failures or punitive repricing, marked SOFR/funding stress, confirmed hyperscaler capex/order cuts, and renewed semiconductor underperformance with deteriorating breadth.
- **De-escalate if:** Oct 30 spread widening reverses or stabilizes on Nov 2/3 official observations, secured funding remains normal, financing completes at sustainable spreads, and semiconductor relative strength and issuer cash-flow guidance recover.
- **Limitations:** FRED credit/VIX observations lag the live US Nov 2 session by at least one trading day. Reuters equity intraday quotes can be delayed. No verified Nov 2 end-of-day IG/HY OAS, real-time SOXX bid/ask or broad fund flows; no precise same-time gas/copper/gold/FX composite. No claim of continuous monitoring or account drawdown.
