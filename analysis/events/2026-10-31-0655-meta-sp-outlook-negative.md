## Independent new action vs already recorded risks
- **New verified action:** S&P Global Ratings changed Meta Platforms' **credit-rating outlook from stable to negative**, citing uncertainty about multiyear AI capex and free operating cash flow. **An outlook revision is NOT an issuer-rating downgrade or a default.** Reuters references S&P's **AA-** issuer-rating leverage threshold, but no contemporaneous reduction of the rating was confirmed in this review. The underlying S&P rating-action document was not independently accessible; Reuters directly reports the action and quotes the agency.
- **New quantified rating-agency scenario, not realized financial results:** S&P forecasts Meta **free operating cash flow (FOCF) deficits of ~$39bn in 2026 and ~$71bn in 2027**, potentially negative beyond 2028. S&P says adjusted leverage could exceed **1.0x in 2027**, its referenced threshold for the AA- rating. These are **agency projections**, not Meta's official guidance or realized losses.
- **Prior evidence, NOT new:** Oct 21 Meta results reported Q3 company-defined FCF **-$2.12bn**, operating cash flow **+$32.79bn**, revenue **$64.60bn (+26% YoY)**, and Sep 30 cash/securities **$82.45bn**; 2026 capex guidance **$135–145bn**. The Oct 26 event `2026-10-26-2148-meta-equity-offering.md` already documented approximately $35bn launched equity/convertible offerings and potential up-to-$60bn future ATM. The Oct 22/23 Meta CDS rise and broader AI financing concerns are already recorded. Do **not** recast old CPI, SOXX declines, or Meta equity financing as new.
- **Difference:** previously investor/market financing concern; now a **formal negative rating-agency outlook action** and a specific adverse multiyear FOCF projection. A rating outlook is a forward-looking risk judgment, not direct evidence that Meta has stopped paying suppliers or canceled chip orders.
