# Supermicro FY2027 Q1 preliminary business update — October 20, 2026

**Research checked:** 2026-10-21 07:51 KST (2026-10-20 18:51 US Eastern). Public market research only; no account information.

## Verified new event (not recycled SOXX/CPI news)
On October 20, 2026, Super Micro Computer (SMCI) **officially** raised its preliminary fiscal Q1 2027 revenue estimate for the quarter ended September 30, 2026:
- **Revenue:** $18.5–19.5 billion vs **previous company guidance $14.5–15.5 billion**. Midpoint increase $4.0 billion, **+26.7%** from $15.0 billion.
- **Gross margin:** **12.0–12.5%** vs previous guidance **10.4–10.8%**, midpoint improvement **+1.65 percentage points**.
- Company attributes the change to higher AI platform demand and customer/product mix.
- **Unaudited, preliminary estimates**; independent auditors have not reviewed the figures. Final earnings call scheduled **November 3, 2026**.

## Interpretation and counterevidence
- **New fundamental data**, unlike generic claims that AI demand is resilient. It suggests a better near-term shipment/revenue picture for at least one AI-server integrator.
- **Not a semiconductor-wide order or profitability guarantee.** Revenue recognition and margins for one server vendor cannot be mechanically applied to SOXX or upstream chip makers. Product/customer concentration, component availability, working capital, AI project financing, and final audit adjustments remain relevant.
- Counterevidence: broader AI financing concerns and elevated rates remain. Reuters Oct 20 reported J.P.
